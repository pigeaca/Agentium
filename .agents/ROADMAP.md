# Roadmap

Product direction: an AI development lab for coding agents. The [feasibility study](../docs/research/2026-09-27-ai-development-lab.md) is accepted as a hybrid strategy ([decision](decisions/2026-09-27-hybrid-strategy.md)). Vision: know what your AI setup really changes, in an afternoon, for a few dollars.

History: [completed plans](plans/archive/INDEX.md) and [decisions](decisions/README.md).

## North star
- **Time to first verdict:** today ~14 commands and 8–20 tasks by hand; target 3 commands, under an hour.
- **Dollars per verdict:** a success verdict costs about $120–180 today; target $60 or less.
