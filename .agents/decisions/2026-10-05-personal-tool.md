# ADR: Agentium is its owner's personal tool

## Status

Accepted by the user on 2026-10-05.

## Context

Two reviews on 2026-10-04 and 2026-10-05 judged Agentium as a product: who would use it, what other tools offer, how to position it. They proposed new directions several times. The user answered: "I dont wanna convert it in commercial product, I want it for me", and earlier: "Its difficult to fit new ideas and chage everthing".

## Decision

- Agentium is built for its owner's own use: to decide things about the owner's own AI setup (models, effort, agents, rules) on the owner's own repositories.
- It is not a commercial product. No work is planned for a market or for customers, or to compete with other tools.
- This record changes nothing about the repository being public: it is shared as is.

## Consequences

- A change is worth doing when it helps the owner answer a question about their own setup, or keeps the tool correct and cheap to keep.
- Reviews and plans do not argue from demand, competitors or positioning. Other tools are a source of ideas, nothing more.
- A new direction needs the owner's ask or evidence from the owner's own runs. An idea alone goes to a plan's "Later" list.
- Work that exists for other people's use (other people's repositories, hosted runs, teams, release polish) needs the owner's explicit ask. Which plans in progress this parks is decided in [the plan that followed](../plans/2026-10-05-quiet-console-useful-tasks.md), not here.
- The north star (time and spend to a decisive verdict) and the trust guard (A/A false verdicts at most 5%) stay.
